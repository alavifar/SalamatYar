# Changelog

## 2.0 preview (cross-platform edition, in `app/`)

- A new edition built with Tauri for Windows, macOS and Linux. The Windows edition 1.3 stays the stable release.
- Same features: eye, movement and water reminders, three reminder styles, 58 activities, five languages, themes, statistics, absence detection, chimes and break music, custom music files, settings preview.
- Changing the language no longer restarts the app.
- Settings and statistics are stored in the user's configuration folder.

## 1.3

- Three reminder styles: full screen, a small window in the corner of the screen, or only a notification next to the Windows clock.
- 39 new suggested activities in all five languages (58 in total). Each reminder shows one or two, without repeats until the whole set has been shown.
- Water reminders now include a hydration tip.
- Settings show a preview the moment they change: reminder style, volume, chime, music and custom music files.
- `SalamatYar.exe` launcher and a ready-to-run download package (`dist/SalamatYar.zip`).

## 1.2

- Five languages: Persian, English, Arabic, Russian and Chinese, with right-to-left and left-to-right layouts.
- All interface text moved to `lang.json`, with an audit tool for translations.

## 1.1

- Dark, light and automatic themes.
- Custom music files for eye breaks and movement breaks.
- Confirmation before restarting the timers.

## 1.0

- Eye breaks (20-20-20 rule), movement breaks and water reminders.
- Chimes and break music synthesized by the app.
- Daily statistics, absence detection and an ergonomics guide with sources.
